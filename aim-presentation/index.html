01

Purpose of the Application

AIM Studio is an AI-powered cinema platform that produces emotional, character-driven films, casts community members into productions, and trains aspiring filmmakers in AI storytelling. We make films about sacrifice, regret, and the moments that matter — using AI not to replace cinema but to enable stories that wouldn't otherwise get told.

The platform supports three audience pathways: watching, auditioning, and learning — each with its own infrastructure, from film distribution and subtitle localization to casting pipelines with AI-assisted scoring and a full training academy.

Data Flow — Subscribe Capture

Silent capture with bot detection and manual admin approval

graph TD
    V1["🌐 Visitor"] -->|submits form| F1["Subscribe API"]
    F1 -->|honeypot + timing checks| BC["Bot Scoring Engine"]
    BC -->|score < 70| DB1[("Database: Subscriber\nactive: false\nbotScore: computed")]
    BC -->|score >= 70| REJECT["❌ Silent Reject"]
    DB1 -->|appears in| AD1["Admin Dashboard\n(pending tab)"]
    AD1 -->|reviews bot score + country| DECIDE{"Approve?"}
    DECIDE -->|yes| ACTIVE[("Subscriber\nactive: true")]
    DECIDE -->|no| DEL["🗑 Delete"]
